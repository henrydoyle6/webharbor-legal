English

Don't Think. Act First. is a local-first study planning app for iPhone. The app does not require an account and does not send your tasks, plans, calendar data or study history to the developer.

Data stored on your device

Your work items, plan sessions, completion history, preferences and optional reminder settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's app storage. If you grant Calendar access, the app reads busy intervals to estimate available study time. It does not save calendar event titles, modify your calendar or upload calendar data.

Network requests

The app has no developer-operated backend, advertising SDK, analytics service or cloud sync. Optional Apple system services, such as App Store services, may make their own requests under Apple's policies. Local notifications are scheduled on your device.

Information you enter

Task titles, due dates, estimates, course names and progress entries remain in the app's local storage. Do not enter sensitive personal information into task titles or notes.

Deletion and retention

You can delete work items and plan data from within the app. Deleting the app removes its local data according to iOS storage rules. We do not retain a copy on a developer server.

Children and students

The app is intended for general student use and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact henry-doyleli@outlook.com.

简体中文

Don't Think. Act First. 是一款本地优先的 iPhone 学习计划 App。无需注册账户，不会将任务、计划、日历数据或学习历史发送给开发者。

设备本地存储

工作项、计划 Session、完成记录、偏好设置和可选提醒设置使用 Apple 的 App 本地存储保存在设备上。如果你授权访问日历，App 只读取忙碌时间段来估算可用学习时间，不保存日历事件标题、不修改系统日历，也不上传日历数据。

网络请求

App 没有开发者运营的后端、广告 SDK、分析服务或云同步。可选的 Apple 系统服务可能按照 Apple 的政策自行发出请求。本地通知在设备上调度。

你输入的信息

任务标题、截止日期、估时、课程名称和进度记录保存在 App 本地。请勿在任务标题或备注中输入敏感个人信息。

删除和保留

你可以在 App 中删除工作项和计划数据。删除 App 后，本地数据将遵循 iOS 的存储规则移除。开发者服务器不会保留副本。

联系我们

隐私问题请联系 henry-doyleli@outlook.com。
