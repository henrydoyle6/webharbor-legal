Don't Think. Act First.Privacy Policy

English

Add a task

Tap Add, choose Due or This Week, enter the title and estimate, then tap Add to Plan. The planner assigns sessions automatically.

Adjust a plan

Use Done, Partly Done or Not Today on Today. The app updates future sessions automatically and offers Undo when a feasible plan changes.

Calendar access

Calendar access is optional. If you allow it, the app uses busy time to avoid overbooking study capacity. You can continue using the app without Calendar access.

Reminders

Notifications are optional and local to your device. You can change notification permission in iPhone Settings.

Need help?

Tell us the app version, iOS version and the action that caused trouble. Screenshots are helpful. Never send passwords, private calendar details or other sensitive information.

简体中文

添加任务

点击 Add，选择 Due 或 This Week，填写标题和估时，再点击 Add to Plan。Planner 会自动分配 Session。

调整计划

在 Today 使用 Done、Partly Done 或 Not Today。App 会自动调整后续 Session；可行调整会提供 Undo。

日历权限

日历权限是可选的。授权后，App 会读取忙碌时间，避免学习容量被超额占用。没有日历权限也可以完整使用 App。

提醒

通知是可选的，仅在设备本地运行。你可以在 iPhone 设置中修改通知权限。

需要帮助？

请提供 App 版本、iOS 版本和出现问题的操作。截图会很有帮助。请勿发送密码、私人日历详情或其他敏感信息。
